\section{Task 3: Pick and Place}

% --- Slide 1: High-Level Coordination ---
\begin{frame}{Task 3: Coordination Strategy}
    The \texttt{coordinator\_task3} node manages the full transport cycle.
    \vspace{0.2cm}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Data Loading:} Loads exact Pick and Place locations saved in Task 2 from \texttt{marker\_locations.csv}.
        \item \textbf{Sequence:} Enforces the mandatory order: Cube \textbf{63} followed by Cube \textbf{582}.
        \item \textbf{Visual Feedback:} Publishes \texttt{target\_cube\_frame} to TF for real-time visualization in RViz.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% --- Slide 2: Manipulation Logic ---
\begin{frame}[fragile]{Task 3: MoveIt2 Integration}
    The \texttt{manipulator} node interfaces with MoveIt2 using the \texttt{RRTConnectkConfigDefault} planner.
    \vspace{0.2cm}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Collision Avoidance:} Added virtual collision boxes (IDs 10-13) to prevent the arm from hitting the floor or robot base.
        \item \textbf{Safety:} Implemented a "Safe Joint Configuration" to retract the arm during navigation.
    \end{itemize}
    \begin{lstlisting}[language=Python]
# From manipulator.py
self.safe_joints_position = [
    0.0, 0.20, -1.34, -0.20, 1.94, -1.57, 1.37, 0.00
]
    \end{lstlisting}
\end{frame}

% --- Slide 3: Grasping Mechanics ---
\begin{frame}{Task 3: Robust Grasping (Gazebo)}
    To ensure stability in the simulation (as suggested in the guidelines), we implemented the \textbf{Link Attacher} plugin logic.
    \vspace{0.2cm}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Approach:} The arm moves to a "Pre-Grasp" pose defined by a $-0.4m$ offset along the X-axis of the marker.
        \item \textbf{Attaching:} Once the gripper closes, we call the \texttt{/ATTACHLINK} service to rigidly bind the cube to the gripper link.
    \end{itemize}
    \begin{center}
        \textit{This prevents the object from slipping during the robot's navigation phases.}
    \end{center}
\end{frame}

% --- Slide 4: Placement Logic ---
\begin{frame}[fragile]{Task 3: Placement \& Release}
    The placement pose is calculated dynamically relative to the detected grasp transform.
    \vspace{0.2cm}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Logic:} We reuse the grasp orientation but apply a vertical offset to ensure the cube is released \textit{on top} of the table.
        \item \textbf{Offset:} $+15cm$ ($0.15m$) on the Z-axis.
    \end{itemize}
    \begin{lstlisting}[language=Python]
# From coordinator_task3.py
release_frame = kdl.Frame(self.base_T_marker)
release_frame.p[2] += 0.15 # Raise Z by 15 cm
    \end{lstlisting}
    After placement, the \texttt{/DETACHLINK} service is called to release the object physics.
\end{frame}
